# Biomedical publication redesign: domain research

Researched and accessed 2026-09-05. Sources below were opened or returned by the live web tool; search snippets were used for discovery, and operational recommendations rest on official guideline pages, source papers, or public repository documentation. This is design research, not a systematic review of agent systems. No external system was installed or run.

## Changes with highest practical value

1. **Separate four judgments.** Identifier resolution establishes which document exists; publication-status checking checks corrections/retractions/version; content verification establishes whether a passage supports a claim; appraisal assesses bias and certainty. A single `verified` flag must not imply all four. Store locator, access date, verification scope, and limits. PubMed links corrections and retractions; Crossmark coverage depends on participating publishers. Absence of a detected notice is not a guarantee of integrity. [D13–D15]
2. **Update the reporting router by actual design.** CONSORT 2025 and SPIRIT 2025 replace older core trial guidance. Clinical prediction models using regression or ML route to **TRIPOD+AI**, not a vague TRIPOD-AI choice. AI diagnostic-accuracy studies can use the published STARD-AI 2025 extension. Keep a dated baseline, reopen official checklist plus relevant extension at use time, and resolve venue instructions explicitly. Do not reproduce hundreds of checklist items in the core skill. [D01–D05]
3. **Reporting is not conduct or validity.** Mark items `addressed`, `partial`, `missing`, `not_applicable` with rationale and manuscript locator. A coverage percentage is not proof of study quality, and an agent passing structural validation does not establish compliance. Preserve protocol version and deviations; rewriting hypotheses into polished rationale must not make post hoc ideas appear prespecified. [D02, D06]
4. **Reviews require a methods workflow.** Save exact queries, platform/database, dates, filters, retrieved/exported counts and deduplication rules; distinguish record, report and study IDs; retain full-text exclusion reasons; reconcile the flow diagram with ledgers. PRISMA reports a review; Cochrane/JBI-type methods govern conduct. Avoid claiming systematic coverage from web search or an arbitrary minimum number of databases. Two LLM agents are not two independent human reviewers. [D06–D10]
5. **Risk of bias and certainty must remain distinct.** Use appropriate study/result-level appraisal (e.g., RoB 2 for randomized results) and outcome-level body-of-evidence certainty (GRADE where suitable). Record supporting judgments, not a score invented from reporting completeness or journal prestige. Do not force GRADE onto every narrative or scoping review. [D09–D11]
6. **Make statistical reporting an explicit lane.** Recover an estimate's population, denominator, units, time point, comparison, adjustment and uncertainty before writing. Separate editorial verification from recalculation and scientific reanalysis. When no reproducible data/code exists, report supplied output with its provenance and clearly limit validation. Do not silently change discrepant n, p, CI, model or endpoint. Preserve null, harmful and exploratory findings. [D12, D16]
7. **Add a submission boundary that fails honestly.** Missing declarations, unresolved journal rules, partial essential reporting coverage, unresolved source contradictions or unverified factual citations block `submission-ready`; they do not block useful draft delivery. Readiness is a documented recommendation with explicit author responsibilities, not a regex score or permission to submit. Separate manuscript preparation, final author attestations and actual external submission. [D16–D19]
8. **Retain publication voice without erasing transparency.** Preserve the old suite's meaning ledger, forest-first structure, internal/public separation, design-matched confidence and humanization safeguards. Refine the blanket association router: randomized and justified causal-inference studies require an explicit estimand and assumptions; statistical adjustment alone does not create causal evidence. User evidence outranks stylistic simplification.

## Comparable systems: useful patterns and rejected patterns

| System inspected | Applicable pattern | Adaptation / limit |
|---|---|---|
| [PaperQA / PaperQA2](https://github.com/Future-House/paper-qa) | Separate search, evidence gathering and answer generation; retain document/page contexts; cache indexed documents; retraction-aware metadata; keep synthetic image/table retrieval captions separate from actual source text. | Adopt bounded evidence packets and publication-status checks. Do not accept retrieval rank, generated summaries or auto-inferred metadata as verification; inspect source text/tables. No dependency on its hosted APIs or models is needed. |
| [K-Dense scientific-writing](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/blob/main/skills/scientific-writing/SKILL.md) | Typed source/claim/consistency records, dated non-scoring reporting selector, deterministic offline checks, explicit declaration states. | Adapt only useful structure. Its mandatory human verification of every factual claim and full scaffold can overburden authorized small edits; retain proportional effort and agent source verification with honest scope. Do not copy its automatic self-citation instruction. |
| [K-Dense literature-review](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/blob/main/skills/literature-review/SKILL.md) | Phase separation from planning to search, screening, extraction, synthesis, reference verification and report assembly; reproducible search log. | Reject arbitrary “minimum 3 databases,” treating general web search as equivalent to a bibliographic database, and prescriptive schematic generation. Search choice should follow the question, study type and method. |
| [K-Dense peer-review](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills/blob/main/skills/peer-review/SKILL.md) | Separate scientific/statistical/policy review; prioritized findings with locations and evidence; response/revision mapping. | Adopt bounded role review and coverage checks; keep confidentiality and actual journal permissions explicit. A simulated reviewer is advisory, not peer-review completion. |
| [Cochrane Handbook 6.5](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current) | Separate prespecified scope, selection, extraction, synthesis, risk of bias, missing evidence, certainty and interpretation; named disagreements and adjudication. | Make this a review-methods module, not a burden on original-research editing. Preserve actual human review provenance and avoid claiming Cochrane-standard conduct when it was not performed. |

## Methods guidance checked on 2026-11-17

The following sources informed the revised routing, statistical reporting and review-methods references. The examples in the skill are synthetic. Recheck the statement, any amendments or extensions, and the target venue when preparing a real submission.

| Decision in the skill | Source consulted |
|---|---|
| Select the trial protocol or results statement by stage. For coded or linked observational data, recover cohort algorithms, validation and linkage details. For an explicitly emulated trial, compare the proposed protocol with its implementation and the alignment at time zero. | [SPIRIT and CONSORT statements](https://www.consort-spirit.org/published-statements), [RECORD statement](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1001885), [TARGET statement](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40903028/) |
| Distinguish post-randomization events from missing observations. A sensitivity analysis examines the same estimand under alternative assumptions. Label a change in the treatment effect being estimated. | [ICH E9(R1), sections III and V](https://www.fda.gov/media/148473/download) |
| Describe the comparator and the direction of agreement when a direct test comparison lacks a reference standard. A hazard ratio and a cumulative incidence at a given time answer different questions in the presence of competing events. | [FDA diagnostic-test guidance, section 4](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/statistical-guidance-reporting-results-studies-evaluating-diagnostic-tests-guidance-industry-and-fda), [Austin and colleagues on competing risks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26858290/) |
| Choose the reporting statement by the target question. For critical appraisal, QUADAS-3 evaluates diagnostic accuracy estimates. PROBAST+AI distinguishes model development and evaluation. | [STARD-AI statement](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40954311/), [TRIPOD+AI statement](https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378), [QUADAS-3 developer resources](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/population-health-sciences/projects/quadas/quadas-3/), [PROBAST+AI statement](https://www.bmj.com/content/388/bmj-2024-082505) |
| Match a review to its protocol, scope and update status. When an intervention review cannot pool estimates, report the synthesis grouping, methods and contributing studies. | [PRISMA-P](https://www.prisma-statement.org/protocols), [PRISMA flow diagrams](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-flow-diagram), [JBI scoping review eligibility](https://jbi-global-wiki.refined.site/space/MANUAL/355862707), [SWiM statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7190266/) |

## Remaining research caveats

- Nature Portfolio's AI policy URL could not be fetched through the web tool because of an authentication redirect; no policy conclusion was drawn from it. Reopen the actual target venue when needed.
- K-Dense pages have current contents and older search-cache descriptions that differ materially. Recommendations above are based on opened GitHub files, not search summaries. The repository was inspected, not executed or independently benchmarked.
- These sources justify a publication-assistance workflow, not a guarantee of clinical/statistical correctness, journal acceptance, misconduct detection, complete literature coverage, or compliance certification.
- Established reporting standards should be selected by study design; a newly published proposal such as an AI review reporting framework should not be elevated to mandatory accepted guidance without a separate validity/adoption check.
