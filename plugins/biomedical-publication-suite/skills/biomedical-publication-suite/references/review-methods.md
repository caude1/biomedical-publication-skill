# Review methods and evidence synthesis

Load for systematic/scoping/narrative reviews, evidence maps, meta-analysis reporting or audits of existing reviews. Use [literature-intelligence.md](literature-intelligence.md) for retrieval and identifier/content verification. A targeted citation search does not require an entire review package.

## Establish the method before the label

Identify question, review type, intended inference, protocol/registration, eligibility, information sources and search dates. Infer a provisional scope and advance supported work; request only missing decisions that change eligibility or synthesis. Never invent a prospective protocol, registration, search date or completed screening. If methods are incomplete, deliver a scoped evidence summary or provisional draft and name the precise missing work.

Set the deliverable by stage. A protocol records the proposed eligibility, search, selection and synthesis with its date and version. For a scoping review, align the population, concept and context with the evidence to be charted. For a completed review, distinguish the planned decisions, documented amendments and methods actually used. For an update, identify the previous review, cutoff, inherited studies and newly searched period. [PRISMA-P](https://www.prisma-statement.org/protocols) · [JBI scoping review methods](https://jbi-global-wiki.refined.site/space/MANUAL/355862707) · [PRISMA flow diagrams](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-flow-diagram)

PRISMA describes reporting. Use a suitable methods framework for conduct, such as the [Cochrane Handbook](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current) for intervention reviews. Choose information sources for the question/eligibility; no fixed database count guarantees completeness. Web search and citation trails supplement database searching but cannot silently substitute for a planned database search.

## Auditable workflow

1. **Scope/protocol.** Fix PICO/PECO or a suitable alternative, designs/settings/time points, outcomes, synthesis groups, appraisal and decision rules. Record amendments and timing. Exploratory analyses remain exploratory.
2. **Search/export.** Save exact executed strategy, database AND platform, date, coverage period, filters/limits, result/export count and export path. Preserve unsuccessful searches/access limits affecting coverage. Use controlled vocabulary/text words where suitable. Search registries, protocols, preprints, grey literature or citation trails when the method calls for them; label source type.
3. **Deduplicate/link.** Keep discovery `record_id`, report `source_id`, and underlying `study_id` where useful. A registry, protocol, primary report, supplement and follow-up may describe one study. Record deduplication must not erase distinct reports. Preserve aliases; do not double-count overlapping cohorts in a pooled estimate.
4. **Screen.** Record actual title/abstract and full-text decisions, decision-maker identity/role, uncertainty and full-text exclusion reason. Resolve disagreements by the prespecified process. Agents may assist; they do not count as two independent human reviewers. For Cochrane intervention reviews, final eligibility requires at least two independent people. If agent or single-person screening occurred, report that honestly. [Cochrane chapter 4](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04)
5. **Extract/verify.** Pilot fields on diverse reports. Anchor material fields to source/version/page/table/section and a brief quotation or transcription when appropriate. Record units, denominator, time point, arm and adjusted/unadjusted analysis. Keep `not_reported`, `unclear`, `needs_full_text` and `conflict` distinct. Do not backfill a primary report from an unchecked review table.
6. **Appraise.** Choose by the question and the result being appraised. RoB 2 addresses a randomized trial result. For primary diagnostic accuracy studies, QUADAS-3 assesses bias and applicability for selected accuracy estimates. For prediction models, PROBAST+AI distinguishes the quality of model development from bias in an evaluation of predictive performance and considers applicability in both. Apply the appropriate intervention or exposure tool to nonrandomized studies. Record domain judgments with supporting evidence and the tool version actually used. [QUADAS-3](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/population-health-sciences/projects/quadas/quadas-3/) · [PROBAST+AI](https://www.bmj.com/content/388/bmj-2024-082505)
7. **Synthesize.** Before pooling, check clinical/methodological compatibility, outcomes, effect measures, dependence, multiple arms, time points and overlapping cohorts. Document choices/departures. Quantitative synthesis requires actual computation from verified extractable data and reproducible code; prose generation cannot supply a pooled effect. If quantitative intervention effects cannot be pooled, report how studies were grouped, which effect measures contributed, the actual synthesis method, and which studies drove each conclusion. Counting significant studies conceals effect size, precision and dependence. Prefer a table or forest plot of study estimates with uncertainty where comparable, even when no summary estimate is justified. [SWiM](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7190266/)
8. **Assess certainty where suitable.** Certainty is outcome-specific across a body of evidence; risk of bias is one input. For GRADE, document risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias judgments, plus applicable upgrading rationale. Do not infer high certainty from n/significance, or force GRADE onto every scoping/narrative review. [Cochrane chapter 14](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-14)
9. **Reconcile/report.** Reconcile records, duplicates, reports sought/not retrieved, full texts excluded and included studies/reports with the flow diagram. Counts come from actual ledgers. Check each included citation's identity, publication status and content support before synthesis. Complete the applicable official checklist with manuscript locations.

## Two checks that change the synthesis

**Count the right unit.** In a synthetic example, 21 reports were sought, three were unavailable, and 18 were assessed. Eleven full-text exclusions leave seven included reports. If those seven describe five distinct studies, report both counts. An unavailable full text contributes to the retrieval count. It does not supply an eligibility judgment. For any pooled outcome, link the actual contributing effects back to studies, arms and time points so multiple publications or shared controls do not silently increase their weight.

**Distinguish an absent result from a zero.** A study that omitted adverse events, a study that monitored events and reported none, and a study whose harms supplement was inaccessible contribute different evidence. Carry those states through extraction, synthesis denominators and discussion. When a pooled estimate exists, report the outcome, contributing studies and participants where available, effect scale, interval, synthesis model and observed heterogeneity from the actual analysis. A random-effects model does not resolve incompatible estimands or duplicate participants.

## Outputs and limits

For compact evidence work, keep `search_log.md`, `literature_register.csv` and `extraction_ledger.csv` as needed. Review-scale work may add screening, study/report linkage, appraisal and synthesis files. Reuse existing project records rather than imposing a competing database.

Final delivery names search cutoff/scope, sources actually searched, access limits, screening/appraisal method, unresolved conflicts and synthesis basis. Keep scientific uncertainty beside the affected conclusion. Never label a partial search exhaustive or a generated summary a completed systematic review.

The [PRISMA 2020](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020), [PRISMA-S](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-search), [PRISMA-ScR](https://www.prisma-statement.org/scoping) and Cochrane Handbook pages were checked on 2026-11-17. The Handbook index listed version 6.5 (2024). Confirm the applicable tool and checklist versions for each new review.
